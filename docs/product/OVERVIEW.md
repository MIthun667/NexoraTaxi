# AI Company Operating System (OS)

The Universal AI Company OS is an enterprise-grade SaaS platform designed to centralize and automate organizational control through live telemetry and autonomous agents.

## Core Operating Domains

The platform is structured around three primary functional pillars:

### 1. Workforce
Manages the entire personnel lifecycle, including onboarding, compliance, availability, and performance. The system treats all contributors as canonical workforce members, providing a unified view of organizational readiness.

### 2. Assets
Provides real-time visibility into managed equipment, vehicles, and systems. Tracking includes maintenance schedules, operational status, and readiness signals to ensure assets are available when demand peaks.

### 3. Operations
The execution layer where assignments are made, work orders are tracked, and incidents are resolved. Operations centralizes live telemetry into a unified command surface for real-time organizational control.

## AI & Governance

- **Command Centers**: High-level telemetry surfaces for Executives and Operational leads.
- **Autonomous Agents**: Specialized AI units that monitor capacity, predict risk, and propose actions.
- **Workflows & Approvals**: A formal governance layer ensuring that all AI-proposed actions and critical operational changes undergo human-in-the-loop review.
